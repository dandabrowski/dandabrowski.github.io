About me

I’m interested in legal alignment, AI constitutionalism, and what AI means for the law: as a subject of law, a consumer of law, and a producer of law. Most of my writing lives on my Substack, Constituitur.

Currently I’m an independent researcher and writer, exploring legal frontiers and technological progress. I’ve spent 7+ years working across law, finance, and tech.

I hold an LL.M. in Financial Law from the Frankfurt School of Finance & Management, an M.A. in East Asian Studies and Economics from the University of Duisburg-Essen, and a B.A. from Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf.

Besides being a father and an explorer of legal frontiers, I’m a passionate conlanger and worldbuilder.
